## Managing Profile Settings

Settings brings together your profile, appearance, sharing, connectors, skills, and secrets. Assistant options and memory are under **Tasks** → **Assistants**.

### Open Settings

- Click your **profile avatar** → **Settings** in the app header
- Or click the **gear** beside the model and thinking pill to open **Profile**, including appearance and language
- Or type `/settings` in chat

### Sections

- **Discover** gives quick links to the main settings areas. Choose **AI assistant** to open your personal assistant’s model, thinking, and memory options.
- **Profile** manages your profile picture, language, and theme
- **Sharing** lists everything you have shared and lets you manage access
- **Tasks** → **Assistants** lets you manage assistant names, models, reasoning, and memory. Your personal assistant is listed first.
- **Memory** on an assistant’s page lets you inspect and edit what it remembers. **Options** controls whether it can learn and save new memories.
- **Capabilities** manages connectors, skills, and secrets

### Model and thinking

The pill at the bottom of Home shows the current model and thinking level. Open it to choose a model or adjust the **Thinking** slider. Drag to a stop, click a level, or use the arrow keys with the slider focused. Changes save automatically when you release the slider or key.

More thinking can help with complex tasks, but may take longer. The available stops depend on the selected model. Models with a fixed thinking level show that level without an adjustable slider.

The same control appears in assistant **Options** and the model settings dialog. On Home it changes your personal assistant; when another assistant is active, it changes that assistant’s settings.

### Appearance and navigation

Choose **Profile** to change your photo, pick a light or dark appearance, or switch between English and Arabic. Theme previews show each appearance before you select it. On a phone, swipe the section buttons at the top to reach every Settings section.

Concierge remembers your appearance in this browser, including on the starting screen. If no appearance is saved here, the starting screen uses your device's light or dark setting until your account preferences load.

Under **Capabilities**, use **Connectors**, **Skills**, or **Secrets** to switch tools. Settings scrolls as one panel; secret names and values stack on narrow screens.

### Secrets

Open **Settings** → **Capabilities** → **Secrets** to add, update, or delete API keys and tokens.

Saved secrets are encrypted and shown as masked password values. To replace a saved value, focus the value field, enter the new value, and click **Save**. After the save is verified, the field returns to a masked value and shows a saved confirmation.

### Tips

- Secret names must use `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE`
- Existing secret values are not revealed after they are saved
- Delete a secret only when you no longer want it available to your workspace
