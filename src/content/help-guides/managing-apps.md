## Managing Applets

Concierge supports applets, built-in tools, and connected services that extend its functionality.

### Browsing Applets

- Click the **Applets** icon in the sidebar **Apps** row, or **Manage Applets** at the bottom of the sidebar
- Or navigate to the **Applet Library** — it opens on **Discover** by default
- Use **Discover** to browse public Applet Store listings
- Use **My Applets** to create, open, share, publish, or edit your own applets
- Use **Workspaces** to open or migrate legacy workspace applets, open regular workspaces, or create a new workspace
- Use **Shared With Me** to open applets other users have shared with you
- Browse app cards to open or edit available applets
- Sidebar additions now happen from sidebar edit mode, not from the Applet Library
- Built-in tools, including Chat, Home, Media, Transcribe, Translate, Write, and Files, can be added back from the sidebar picker. Applets opens from the **Apps** row icon instead of as a sidebar list item.
- Use the **⋮ menu** beside Home in the app header → **Edit** to customize the layout: add groups, add applet and automation cards, drag groups and cards into order, remove cards, or resize cards between mini and large. New digest cards can no longer be added, but existing digest cards keep working.
- A specific home applet still overrides the card grid; unset it to use the mixed Home layout
- Applet cards can include generated or edited metadata such as an image, badge label, category, tags, version state, and launch action

### Reordering Sidebar Items

- Use the floating **Edit sidebar** pencil button near the bottom of the sidebar
- Drag sidebar items into the order you want
- Click the **X** on a sidebar item to remove it
- Click **Add** at the bottom of the editable sidebar list to open the picker for built-in tools and your applets
- Filter the picker if needed, toggle one or more items to **Added**, then click **Add** to add them together
- Click **Cancel** or **X** to close the picker without adding anything
- Click the floating check button to leave edit mode
- **New Chat**, **Home**, **Tasks**, **Files**, and **Chats** stay fixed at the top; **Chats** lists recent conversations inline; its chevron collapses the list to free space for apps — click the **Chats** header for full history
- Other installed apps and applets appear under **Apps** and can be reordered in edit mode; click the **Apps** header row to open your applets library
- Installed applets open the published version when one exists, otherwise the latest saved version, otherwise the current Draft

### Connecting Services

- Browse the list of available integrations
- Click on a service to see its description and requirements
- Follow the setup instructions to connect it

### Applet Features

Connected services and applets may add:

- New sidebar navigation items
- Additional AI capabilities
- Specialized workflows and tools

### Tips

- Applets and services you connect or install are specific to your account
- Remove sidebar items you no longer need from sidebar edit mode
- Some apps may require additional permissions or API keys
