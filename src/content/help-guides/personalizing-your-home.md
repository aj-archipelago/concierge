## Personalizing Your Home Page

Keep your apps and your colleagues’ task reports together on **Home**. The header gives you **Add to Home**, **Arrange**, **Custom home page**, and **Take a tour**.

Saved digest cards from the earlier Home page appear automatically alongside your current groups, applets, and automations. Their prompts and reports are preserved. Your next layout change saves the restored cards; if you remove one afterward, it stays removed.

Generated widget backgrounds are reused when you reload Home or open another tab. Concierge retains background-image tasks if you leave while they run. If a background fails, the widget keeps its fallback instead of starting another generation on each visit.

Saved app cover images renew their temporary access links automatically when needed, including in local development. You do not need to regenerate a cover because its link expired.

If rebuilding an existing digest fails, its last saved report stays visible and the task reports the generation error. Edit a missing or empty prompt before rebuilding.

### Add an app or report

Choose **Add to Home** in the header, or **Add** inside a group. The same dialog opens whether Home is empty or already has cards.

- **Describe what you'd like** to create an app or a task for a colleague. If something similar already exists, choose **Use existing** or **Create new**.
- Under **Existing**, choose an app or a task. A task adds a report card showing its latest result.
- For an app, choose **Shortcut** to open it when selected, or **Use on Home** to use it directly on the page.

New apps show their name while they get ready. You can keep using Home and add other items during this time. If an app cannot load, choose **Retry**. Completed Home views are saved and reused, and reloading Home does not start another copy of the same preparation. Saved backgrounds are also reused.

A digest refresh may take several minutes. If it fails, the card keeps its previous report and update time. You can try refreshing the card again.

### Open and manage cards

Select a shortcut to open the app. Apps you use directly on Home and full report cards have an **Open** button in their header. Both open over Home, keeping your place on the page.

Use the **Close (×)** button in the top corner to return to Home. Browser **Back** closes an open view; **Forward** reopens a view closed with Back. **Escape** closes the view when focus is on its surrounding controls. Inside an app, keyboard shortcuts belong to that app.

Every card has **Card options** at the upper end of the card: the right in English and the left in Arabic. Use it to:

- Open the app or report.
- Switch an app between **Show as shortcut** and **Use on Home**.
- Switch a report between **Show summary** and **Show full card**.
- **Edit** the app or report, or **Remove from Home**.

Report cards also offer **Run now** for tasks or **Refresh** for older reports. The update time is a status label. A failed refresh keeps the previous report available.

### Arrange Home

Choose **Arrange** to move cards and groups, rename groups, or change how cards appear. Apps are inactive while you arrange them, so dragging a card does not trigger its controls. Choose **Add group** to create a section and **Done** to return to normal use. Layout changes save as you make them and follow your account across devices.

### Edit an app or report

Choose **Edit** from a card’s menu. App editing opens chat beside the preview. Shortcuts open the full-page draft; apps used directly on Home open their Home view. Save your changes in the editor before choosing **Close**. Returning to Home does not save an unfinished preview. **Clear chat** clears the editor conversation.

A report’s **Edit** dialog lets you change its title. Older prompt-based reports also let you change their instructions. They use your interface language unless the instructions request another language. To change a task’s schedule or instructions, use its **Edit task** link.

### Use a custom home page

Choose **Custom home page** to replace the card layout with a single app. On that page, **Use standard Home** returns to your cards. You can also set an app as Home from the app library.

If rebuilding an existing digest fails, its last saved report stays visible and the task reports the generation error. Edit a missing or empty prompt before rebuilding.

Existing digest cards keep their latest saved report while a refresh waits or runs. Repeated refresh requests for the same card are combined. Editing or removing a card prevents an older refresh from replacing that change. New accounts start without an automatic news digest card; your existing cards and Home layout are preserved.

Digest refreshes preserve cards you edit or remove while a report is being generated. Large source collections retain their full citation text when saved and reopened.

Choose **Take a tour** in the Home header whenever you want a walkthrough.
