## Getting Started with Concierge

Concierge is your AI-powered assistant for content creation, translation, transcription, and more. Here's how to get up and running quickly.

### Your Home Page

When you log in, you'll land on the **Home** page — a customizable dashboard of the applets and tasks you use most. Use the **⋮ menu** beside **Home** in the app header to enter customize mode, or to replay the guided tour. You can also set one of your applets as Home from the **Applets** page, or ask Concierge to set the current applet as Home after building it. Home renders that applet's editable Draft, so updates to the applet can show on Home without publishing it.

### Navigation

Use the **sidebar** on the left to navigate between features:

- **New Chat**, **Home**, **Tasks**, and **Files** stay at the top for quick access
- **Tasks** shows a blue unread indicator when a task result is ready; open **Tasks** → **Recent** to review outputs
- **Chats** lists a few recent conversations inline; use its chevron to collapse or expand the list and leave more room for apps. Your choice is remembered in this browser; click the **Chats** header (or its chat icon) for full history
- **Apps** holds your other installed tools and applets (for example Media, Translate); click the **Apps** header row to open your applets library
- **Applet Library** — Discover public applets, and use or create custom applets via the **Apps** header or **Manage Applets**

### Shared page header

The app header holds the current page title and its main actions. Home editing, library tabs, chat actions, and other page tools use this same space. What’s New, notifications, and your account stay at the other end. On small screens, page actions wrap within the header. The layout mirrors in Arabic. What’s New shows **9+** for more than nine unseen updates; header badges widen to keep longer counts readable.

### Quick Tips

- Use your **profile avatar** → **Settings** to personalize Concierge — choose your AI model, language, theme, manage memory, connectors, and skills
- Type **`/`** in any chat input to see available commands
- The desktop sidebar is compact by default. Hover over it to slide it over the page, or use the pin icon to keep it expanded with dedicated page space.
- Use **Edit sidebar** to add built-in apps or applets, reorder installed apps, and remove items you do not need
- Use the **bell icon** to check background tasks and notifications. Its badge counts active tasks and unread notifications, and pulses only while tasks are pending or running. Finished tasks remain available in the panel and history without keeping the pulse active.
- The **chat icon** in the header opens a docked chat panel alongside any page
- Use **Send feedback** in the sidebar to report an issue, share an idea, or ask a question. You can include a screenshot; Concierge also sends the current page context so the team can investigate.
- Concierge can also submit feedback from chat when the assistant notices a confusing or broken workflow. If the assistant already has a relevant image available, it can attach that image without asking to capture your screen.

Feedback is saved in the admin feedback inbox and notifies the team in Google Chat. A notification delivery problem does not remove your saved report.
