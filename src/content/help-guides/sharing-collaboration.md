## Sharing and Collaboration

Use sharing when you want specific people to open or collaborate on your work without publishing it for everyone.

### What You Can Share

- Chats
- Workspaces
- Applets and selected published applets
- Automations and automation output
- Articles

### Share With People

1. Open the item you want to share
2. Click **Share**
3. Search for a Concierge user
4. For applets, automations, and articles, choose **Viewer** or **Editor**. Chats and workspaces are always shared as view-only.
5. Save the share

You can search for people by their first or last name when Concierge has received that profile information from Microsoft Entra, or by their email address. The people picker hides legacy accounts without an email and shows capitalization-only variants of the same email once.

Viewers can open the shared item. Editors can make changes or continue work. Chats and workspaces are always shared as view-only; recipients can use **Copy and continue** to make their own editable copy.

### Share With a Link

Some resources support link sharing. Use link sharing when anyone with the link should be able to view the item. Turn the link off when you no longer want that access available.

### Find Shared Work

- Open **Settings → Sharing** to review and manage items you have shared
- Open shared-with-me areas, such as **Applet Library → Shared With Me**, to find items shared with you
- Use inbox notifications to open new shares when someone invites you

### Chat Sharing

Shared chats are always view-only. Viewers can read the conversation and use **Copy and continue** to create their own copy and keep working separately. Shared workspaces are also always view-only; recipients use **Copy and continue** to get an editable copy.

Copied chats do not include access to files from the original shared chat unless those files are shared separately.

### Applet Sharing and Publishing

Sharing an applet draft is different from publishing it. Draft shares invite specific people to view or edit the working applet. Publishing creates a live version with its own sharing controls. When publishing, choose **Anyone with the link** for a public URL that is not listed in the Applet Store, **Specific people** to invite selected users to the published version, or **Applet Store** to list it for everyone on the platform. Republishing a later version with link or recipient access does not remove an existing Applet Store listing; unlist from the store when you manage the published applet. An unlisted published applet only opens from its published link when link access is enabled or the viewer was invited.

Installing an applet in your sidebar is private to your account and does not publish or share it.

Applet cover images and shared-file download links refresh using your current access. Sharing an applet does not grant access to its owner's private files or another viewer's personal applet files. Workspace shares allow reading shared files, not changing them.

### Notifications

When someone shares a resource with you, Concierge adds an inbox notification. Use the bell icon to review unread notifications and open the shared item.
