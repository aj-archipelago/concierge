## Sharing and Collaboration

Concierge lets you share selected work with other users or by link, depending on the item and the access options available in your workspace.

### What You Can Share

You can share supported items from their own pages:

- Chats
- Workspaces
- Applets
- Automations
- Articles

### Sharing an Item

1. Open the item you want to share
2. Click **Share**
3. Choose whether to enable link access or add specific people
4. Pick the access level when the item supports more than one role
5. Save your changes

### Reviewing Shared Items

Use the **Sharing** tab in Personalization to review items you own that currently have sharing enabled.

- Filter by item type
- Open the shared item
- Use **Share** again to update people or link access

### Tips

- Use link access when anyone with the link should be able to open the item
- Add specific people when access should stay limited
- Revisit shared items regularly and remove access you no longer need
